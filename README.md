## About

AI scientist with 6+ years of experience developing and translating AI tools for clinical applications, with expertise in deep learning, foundation models, multimodal data, vision-language models, and reproducible HPC-based workflows. I've built models across pathology, radiology, retinal imaging, dermatology, and cardiac cine imaging, taking them from development and multi-institutional validation to prospective evaluation in hospital workflows.

## Current Role

Research Data Analyst at [Indiana University's Division of Computational Pathology](https://medicine.iu.edu/pathology/research/computational-pathology) (Dec 2023 – Present), working on histopathology AI for prognosis and diagnosis applications:

- Trained WHO-aligned diagnostic and prognostic models on 2,500+ cases from >25 clinical sites.
- Built reproducible HPC workflows for clinical translation; delivered a tool now in prospective validation.
- Contribute to NIH and other externally funded research proposals.

Previously: Research Specialist B at the University of Pennsylvania (2022–2023), Assistant System Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services (2021–2022), and Data Science Intern at Nymo.ai (2020).

## Interests
